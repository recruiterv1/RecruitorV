# My Earnings - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 What is My Earnings?

The "My Earnings" feature allows recruiters to track their commission earnings from successful candidate placements. It shows all candidates with "Offer Accepted" or "Joining" status and calculates the estimated commission based on CTC and commission percentage.

## 🚀 Quick Access

1. **Login** as a recruiter
2. Navigate to **"My Earnings"** in the sidebar (💰 icon)
3. View your earnings breakdown

## 📊 What You'll See

### Summary Cards
- **Total Estimated Earnings**: Your total commission from all accepted offers
- **Successful Placements**: Number of candidates with accepted offers
- **Average Commission**: Average commission per placement

### Earnings Table
Shows for each successful placement:
- Position Name
- Candidate Name
- CTC (in Lakhs)
- Commission %
- **Commission Amount** (calculated)
- Status

### Total at Bottom
- **Estimated Earnings**: Sum of all commission amounts

## 💰 Commission Calculation

```
Commission Amount = (CTC Start × Commission %) / 100
```

**Example:**
- Position: Senior Developer
- CTC Start: ₹10 Lakhs
- Commission: 8%
- **Your Commission: ₹80,000**

## 📱 Features

✅ **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile  
✅ **Real-time Data** - Refresh button to update earnings  
✅ **Detailed Breakdown** - See earnings per position  
✅ **Status Tracking** - Only shows "Offer Accepted" and "Joining" candidates  
✅ **Currency Formatting** - All amounts in Indian Rupees (₹)

## 🔍 Understanding Your Earnings

### What's Included?
Only candidates with these statuses:
- ✅ **Offer Accepted**
- ✅ **Joining**

### What's NOT Included?
Candidates with other statuses like:
- ❌ Submitted
- ❌ In Review
- ❌ Rejected
- ❌ Offer Declined

## 📝 Important Notes

1. **Estimated Earnings**: These are calculated estimates based on CTC Start and Commission %
2. **Actual Payout**: May vary based on your agreement with the company
3. **Read-Only**: This page is for viewing only - no data can be modified
4. **Privacy**: You can only see your own earnings, not other recruiters'

## 🔄 Refresh Data

Click the **"Refresh"** button in the top-right corner to update your earnings with the latest data.

## 📱 Mobile View

On mobile devices:

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Why don't I see any earnings?
**A:** You need to have candidates with "Offer Accepted" or "Joining" status. Keep submitting quality candidates!

### Q: How is commission calculated?
**A:** Commission = (CTC Start × Commission %) / 100

### Q: When will I receive payment?
**A:** Payment terms depend on your agreement. This page shows estimated earnings only.

### Q: Can I export this data?
**A:** Export functionality is planned for a future update.

### Q: Why is my total different from what I expected?
**A:** Ensure you're looking at the correct status. Only "Offer Accepted" and "Joining" candidates are included.

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### No Data Showing?
1. Verify you have candidates with "Offer Accepted" status
2. Click the Refresh button
3. Check your internet connection
4. Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+R)

### Wrong Commission Amount?
1. Verify the CTC Start value in the position
2. Check the commission percentage
3. Calculation: (CTC × Commission%) / 100

### Page Not Loading?
1. Clear browser cache
2. Try a different browser
3. Check if you're logged in
4. Contact support if issue persists

## 📞 Support

If you encounter any issues:
1. Clear browser cache
2. Check browser console for errors (F12)
3. Take a screenshot of the error
4. Contact technical support
