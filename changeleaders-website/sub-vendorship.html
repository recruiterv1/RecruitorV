Recruitment Sub-Vendor Partnership — transparent fee slabs and performance-linked incentives for Tech & Non-Tech hiring partners.

Home / Collaborate / Sub Vendorship

How this engagement works

As a Recruitment Sub-Vendor, you work on Tech and/or Non-Tech mandates. Fees and recruiter payouts are linked to monthly joinings, CTC slabs, and performance on submissions & drops.

  • Tech: Recruitment Fee + Recruiter Cost
  • Non-Tech: Service Fee + Recruiter Cost
  • Incentives: Speed, Quality & Drop-Rate linked
  • Fee linked to monthly joinings · Recruiter cost after 30 days · Split payments till 90 days

"We grow when you grow — structured payments, clear incentives, and high-trust collaboration."

Payment Terms (Tech)

  • Recruiter Cost is paid after your candidate completes 30 days in the organisation.
  • Recruitment Fee: 50% after the candidate completes 30 days, remaining 50% after 90 days.

Recruitment Fee for Non-Tech domain

  • Candidate CTC above ₹20L: 50% after 30 days, remaining 50% after 90 days.
  • Candidate CTC between ₹10L–₹20L: 50% after 60 days, remaining 50% after 90 days.
  • Candidate CTC between ₹4L–₹10L: 100% paid after 90 days.
