About the Program

Recruitment is a professional skill developed through real hiring exposure, structured guidance, and outcome ownership — not classroom theory alone. The Select – Train – Hire Program is a selective industry–academia collaboration designed to prepare MBA HR final-year students for careers in recruitment and talent acquisition, while enabling Changeleaders to identify and develop job-ready HR professionals.

More than an internship

This program is not a conventional internship. It is a performance-driven career pathway that runs parallel to academics and does not interfere with campus placements.

Participation & College Association

How Colleges Can Participate

  • Colleges must formally reach out to Changeleaders to participate.
  • Direct student applications to Changeleaders will not be entertained.
  • Any student reaching out directly will be redirected to Recruiterv.com for general information.

Participation is enabled only through institutional association.

Expectations from Colleges

  • Nominate serious and career-focused MBA HR students.
  • Ensure student availability during weekly live coaching sessions.
  • Provide academic alignment and support where required.

No infrastructure support is required from the college.

Student Readiness & Selection

  • Each student must have a laptop, stable internet, and reliable power access.
  • Selection is conducted entirely online — no campus visits for evaluation.
  • All interactions, evaluations, and onboarding are handled virtually.

Cohort Structure

From each participating college, only five (5) students are selected. The cohort operates as a rotational leadership unit: 5 selected MBA HR students + 1 monthly Student Lead (rotates every month). Each month one student acts as Student Lead — building leadership exposure, shared ownership, and strong team accountability.

Who the Program Is Designed For

Eligible Students
  • MBA – Human Resources (2nd Year)
  • Committed to building a career in recruitment
  • Seeking final placement in the recruitment industry
  • Willing to commit 4–5 focused hours per week for 6 months
Not Recommended For
  • Students planning higher education immediately
  • Students preparing for government jobs
  • Students planning to start a business
  • Students looking only for an internship certificate

This is a career-track program, not a resume filler.

Program Model: Select · Train · Hire

1. Select

Students are selected based on communication skills and confidence, a clear focus on a recruitment career, seamless availability for required hours, and willingness to commit for the full 6 months. Selection is merit-based and highly selective.

2. Train

Training is delivered through live recruitment assignments, not simulations: one live job role every week — requirement understanding, candidate sourcing & HR screening, interview coordination and follow-ups. Weekly 1-hour live coaching (typically Friday/Saturday afternoons). Average commitment: 4–5 hours/week.

3. Hire

Performance is evaluated through verified candidate joinings during the program. Career outcomes with Changeleaders are merit-based and linked to actual hiring results.

Hire — Merit-Based Career Outcomes

Placement opportunities with Changeleaders are determined by the number of successful joinings a student drives during the 6-month program.

Successful joiningsPlacement (per annum)
3–5 successful joinings₹3,00,000
6 or more successful joinings₹4,00,000

Final designation on placement: Associate Consultant – Recruitment. All offers are strictly performance-based, subject to ethical conduct and full program completion, and independent of campus placement processes.

Incentives & Recognition

  • ₹5,000 incentive for every successful candidate joining.
  • Letter of Recommendation for students achieving at least one successful joining in a month.
  • Program Completion Certificate for students who complete the full 6-month program.

Program Duration & Commitment

  • Total duration: 6 months, running alongside academic schedules.
  • Examination leaves will be provided.
  • Early Exit Policy: students exiting before completing the full duration will not be eligible for the Program Completion Certificate, Letter of Recommendation, or placement consideration.

Transparency & Governance

Changeleaders maintains strict professional standards to protect the interests of colleges, students, and candidates: no misuse of college or student brand, no interference with campus placements, no false promises to candidates, and transparent tracking of all outcomes.

Important Disclaimer: This is a merit-based, career-aligned program and not a guaranteed placement initiative. Career opportunities are offered only to students who demonstrate consistent performance, drive verified candidate joinings, and successfully complete the full program.

Colleges and institutions interested in collaborating on the Select – Train – Hire Program are invited to connect with us. Our team will be happy to discuss program alignment, student nomination, and next steps.
