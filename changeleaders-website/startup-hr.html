What Recruitment includes

  • Understanding requirements and helping you write a standard Job Description.
  • Co-creating basic Employer Branding & an Elevator Pitch to attract candidates.
  • Talent sourcing and job posting on your behalf.
  • Answering calls on your behalf and coordinating with recruitment consulting firms.

What Onboarding includes

  • Paperwork and compliance — tax declarations, appointment letter, and handing over company policies.
  • Ensuring PF & ESIC compliance / audit of employment compliance.
  • Ensuring compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
